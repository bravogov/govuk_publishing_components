# United Hampshire organisation identity asset mapping

The GOV.UK organisation-logo component remains the implementation authority. The permitted United Hampshire asset mapping is:

- `single-identity`, `eo` and `org`: United Hampshire Government Coat of Arms.
- `ho`: Interior Office Coat of Arms.
- `portcullis`: United Hampshire Parliament logo, including the Offices of the Leaders of the Houses / Business Managers when that crest is selected in Whitehall.
- `hcg`: upstream United Kingdom Government single-identity Coat of Arms, for Hampshire County Government departments.
- `gds`: unchanged upstream GDS crown.
- `no-identity`: no crest.

The Lord Lieutenant's Office logo is deliberately not included in the GOV.UH deployable asset bundle and must not be added or selected on GOV.UH without explicit approval.

Organisation records and crest selections remain editor-controlled through Whitehall. Source code supplies only the permitted identity assets and component options; it must not manufacture or rewrite organisation records.
